<section style="padding-top:0;">
  <div class="eyebrow">Workshop</div>
  <h2>"I want to build this, but it doesn't exist."</h2>
  <p>Workshop is where FRIGUSON takes on design and fabrication commissions. We bring the same thermal-fluid experimental apparatus design and precision instrumentation know-how we've used to build our own cooling technology, and turn your early-stage experimental idea into a working apparatus that produces real data.</p>
</section>

<section class="soft">
  <div class="eyebrow">Services</div>
  <h2>Scope of Services</h2>
  <div class="grid grid-3">
    <div class="card">
      <h3>Custom Apparatus Design &amp; Fabrication</h3>
      <p>We design and build thermal-fluid experimental apparatus tailored to your research goals, from Cold Plate prototypes to heat-transfer test setups.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="card">
      <h3>High-Load Test System Setup</h3>
      <p>We build thin-film-heater-based test systems that let you verify performance under high thermal-load conditions.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="card">
      <h3>Precision Temperature &amp; Flow Instrumentation</h3>
      <p>We configure thermocouple- and DAQ-based precision temperature measurement systems, along with flow and pressure control equipment.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="card">
      <h3>Nano/Micro Surface Fabrication</h3>
      <p>We fabricate experimental prototypes using nano/micro surface manufacturing and porous structure design techniques.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="card">
      <h3>Coolant Purity Management</h3>
      <p>We design coolant purification and filtration equipment to support long-term test reliability.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="card">
      <h3>Proof-of-Concept Consulting</h3>
      <p>From experimental design guidance to data verification, we help design your Proof-of-Concept (PoC) process end to end.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>
